When I'm not doing design work, you can find me helping out as a UTA at the Purdue ECE Shop or handling finances as the treasurer for the Purdue chapter of the Semiconductor Student Alliance.

I've been fortunate to get some great hands-on industry experience over the last couple of summers. Before that, I spent the summer of 2025 as a Product Marketing Engineering Intern at Texas Instruments, learning how to bridge the gap between deep technical product development and market strategy.
